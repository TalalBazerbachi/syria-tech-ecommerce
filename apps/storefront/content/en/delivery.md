# Delivery and payment

## Delivery areas

We currently deliver by courier to **Damascus and Rif Dimashq**. More governorates are being added; the delivery options available for your address are shown at checkout.

## Delivery time and fee

- The delivery fee and expected time are shown on the checkout page before you confirm the order.
- The courier calls the mobile number on the order before arriving.

## Cash on delivery

No bank card needed. Check the order when the courier arrives and pay the amount in cash, in the currency stated on the invoice. Nothing is charged before delivery.

## Tips for a faster delivery

- Enter a valid Syrian mobile number (09XXXXXXXX).
- Mention the nearest landmark (mosque, school, well-known shop) to help the courier.
