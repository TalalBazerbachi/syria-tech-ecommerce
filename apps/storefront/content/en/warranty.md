# Warranty

Every product we sell is genuine and covered by a warranty. The length and type of warranty (distributor warranty or store warranty) are stated in each product's description and on your invoice.

## What the warranty covers

- Manufacturing defects and faults that appear under normal use during the warranty period.
- Repair, or replacement with an equivalent part, at the service centre's discretion.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Physical damage, liquid damage or damage caused by power surges.
- Devices opened or repaired outside authorised service centres.
- Consumable accessories (external batteries, cases, cables) unless stated otherwise.

## Your legal rights

Our warranty does not replace or restrict your rights under Syrian consumer protection law. No contract or invoice can limit the warranty terms the law provides.

## How to make a claim

1. Keep your invoice and the product box.
2. Contact us on WhatsApp or the phone number on your invoice and describe the fault.
3. We collect the device or you drop it off, and we let you know the outcome once it has been inspected.
