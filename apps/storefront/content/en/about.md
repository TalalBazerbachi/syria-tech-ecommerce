# About us

**Syria Tech** is a Syrian online store for computers and their accessories: laptops, accessories, networking gear, storage and audio.

## What we stand for

- **Genuine products** with a clear warranty stated on every product page.
- **Listed prices** with no surprises; the delivery fee is shown before you confirm.
- **Courier delivery** in Damascus and Rif Dimashq, paid in cash on delivery.
- **Exchange within three days** and refunds for defective products, as the law provides.

For questions and orders, reach us on WhatsApp or the phone number shown in the site footer and on your invoice.
