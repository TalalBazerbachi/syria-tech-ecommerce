# Exchanges and refunds

## Exchanges

You may exchange a product **once, within three days** of receiving it, provided it is in its original condition with the complete box, accessories and invoice, and has not been used.

## Refunds

If a product is **defective**, we refund the full amount or replace it with a working one, whichever you prefer. We collect the defective item at our expense.

Refunds are not available for a change of mind after use or when the original packaging is damaged.

## How to request one

1. Contact us within three days of delivery on WhatsApp or the phone number on your invoice.
2. Tell us the product, the order number and the reason for the exchange or refund.
3. The courier collects the item and hands you the replacement or the cash once its condition is confirmed.

These terms follow Syrian consumer protection law and do not reduce any right the law gives you.
